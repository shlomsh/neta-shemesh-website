# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Couples and families in the Sharon region of Israel (primarily Netanya) considering therapy. Visitors are in a state of emotional vulnerability — they arrive with hesitation, looking for safety signals before making contact. The primary job: decide whether to trust Netta enough to book a first session.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand site for Netta Shemesh, a licensed clinical social worker and certified couples/family therapist. The site communicates professional credibility, warmth, and safe containment. Success = a visitor converts to a phone call, WhatsApp message, or email inquiry.

## Brand Personality

Warm. Grounded. Trustworthy.

The site should feel like the practitioner herself: emotionally intelligent, approachable, and quietly confident — not clinical or formal. The experience should lower anxiety, not add to it.

## Anti-references

- Sterile medical/clinic aesthetics (white walls, cold blues, stock stethoscopes)
- Generic "coach/guru" landing pages (bold claims, countdown timers, social proof inflation)
- Over-designed portfolio sites that put design before human warmth

## Design Principles

1. **Safety before persuasion.** Every visual and copy decision should reduce hesitation, not push. A visitor who feels safe will self-convert; pressure has the opposite effect.
2. **Warmth through restraint.** The brand palette (plum, mauve, blush, cream) and Elamy display font carry emotional warmth — don't over-animate or over-decorate on top of it.
3. **Credibility is earned in specifics.** Credentials, years of experience, location — concrete details that a hesitant client needs to see. Abstract platitudes undermine trust.
4. **RTL is first-class.** Hebrew is the primary language. Every layout, spacing, and reading-direction decision must be designed for RTL first.
5. **Contact is the conversion.** Every section leads toward contact — WhatsApp, phone, or email. Friction-free access to contact is the most important UX goal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Hebrew RTL throughout. Users may include people in emotional distress — clear, calm, readable typography is essential. No aggressive motion. `prefers-reduced-motion` support is required.
